Who We Are

Inosoft has built trusted solutions since 2006, empowering clients across nations.

Founded in 2006 in Surabaya, Inosoft began as a boutique development house with a vision to bridge local Indonesian talent with global technology standards. Over 20 years, we've evolved into a trusted offshore development center serving clients across 10+ countries.

Our journey from a small team to 100+ expert engineers reflects our commitment to growth, innovation, and delivering software that truly scales with our clients' ambitions.

OUR ENGINEERING APPROACH

We choose technology based on what the system needs

We are not tied to a single stack or trend. We choose technology based on the complexity of the product, the performance required, the pace of delivery, and the long-term maintainability of the system.

Architecture before tools

We start with business needs and system requirements first, then select the stack that best supports performance, clarity, and long-term growth.

Custom backend systems

PHP, Node.js and Go form the core of much of our backend work — from structured business applications to scalable services and integrations.

Modern frontend delivery

We use React and Vue.js to build interfaces that are fast, maintainable, and appropriate for the product’s complexity.

Pragmatic low-code use

We use Microsoft Power Apps when it is the right fit for internal tools, workflows, and faster delivery — without making it the foundation for everything.
