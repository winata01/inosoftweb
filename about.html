THE HISTORY OF INOSOFT

Inosoft has built trusted solutions since 2006, empowering clients across nations.

Founded in 2006 in Surabaya, Inosoft began as a boutique development house with a vision to bridge local Indonesian talent with global technology standards. Over 20 years, we've evolved into a trusted offshore development center serving clients across 10+ countries.

Our journey from a small team to 100+ expert engineers reflects our commitment to growth, innovation, and delivering software that truly scales with our clients' ambitions.

2006

Inosoft was established and began building its foundational software solutions.

2007

Launched softwaresekolah.com as SaaS product and expanded into the education technology market.

2011

Our SaaS platform reached 50,000 users, marking a major growth milestone.

2016

Established Virtusee Peta Sukses as a subsidiary, expanding our business portfolio.

2018

Established Sistem Sekolah Sukses as another subsidiary to strengthen our education ecosystem.

2021

Launched Arata Mart, a collaboration between Pesan Lokal and AMH Modern Market.

2022

Launched the Inosoft Bootcamp to develop new tech talents.

2024

INTI was established as the official holding company for Inosoft Group.
